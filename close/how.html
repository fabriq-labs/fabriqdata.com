You send the month. We close it. You sign off.

The books stay in QuickBooks, Zoho Books, or Xero. We work the month on top of them. Nothing is written back until a person approves it.

  1. Connect

    The ledger, the bank feeds, the bills, and the invoices come into one month.

  2. Prepare

    Bills are read, spending is coded, and each bank line is matched to what it paid.

  3. Approve

    A bookkeeper reviews the month. You approve what hits the books.

AI categorization

Spending is coded to your chart of accounts as it lands. A bookkeeper looks at anything that is not a clear match.

Document capture

Invoices, bills, and statements are read from the PDF or the photo. The month does not start with retyping.

Agentic reconciliation

Each bank and card line is matched to the account and the document it came from. The match waits until a person approves it.

Accounts payable

Every bill is captured, coded, and matched to the payment. What is still open stays in the month until it clears. This is the bookkeeping. It is not a bill-pay product.

Agentic close

An agent lays out the month: what to book, what to categorize, what to reconcile. A bookkeeper reviews it. You approve what hits the books.

Connectors

Your books stay in the ledger you already use. Banks, bills, and invoices sit beside them. We write back only what has been approved.
